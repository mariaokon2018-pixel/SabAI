About Us

Tony's Italian Kitchen has been serving the community since 1998. Our chef, Tony Rossi, trained in Naples and brings authentic Italian flavors to every plate. We use only the freshest ingredients — our pasta is made daily, our pizza dough rises for 48 hours, and our sauces simmer all day.

Whether you're joining us for a romantic dinner, a family celebration, or a quick lunch, we promise an unforgettable dining experience. Buon appetito!
